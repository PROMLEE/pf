These SVGs are served locally so portfolio symbols are not sent to a third-party image service during page loads.

- `google.svg`, `samsung.svg`, `hyundai.svg`, `qualcomm.svg`: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), version 16. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners. See the [Simple Icons disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md).
- `btc.svg`, `eth.svg`, `sol.svg`: [Cryptocurrency Icons](https://github.com/spothq/cryptocurrency-icons), CC0-1.0.

All other holdings use a local symbol badge. Add a verified logo here and map its ticker in `app/AssetIcon.tsx` to expand coverage.
